# Welcome to PESU Playbook!

**Your Ultimate Guide to the Unified Academic Planner**

PESU Playbook is your central hub for all academic events, deadlines, and schedules for your department. This guide will walk you through how to get the most out of the platform.

---

## 1. Getting Started: Finding Your Calendar

When you first visit PESU Playbook, you'll see a welcome page with a list of all available academic departments.

- **Simply click on your department's name** to access its dedicated calendar.

This ensures you are only seeing events that are relevant to you.

---

## 2. Navigating the Calendar

Once you've selected your department, you'll see the main calendar view. Here’s how to navigate it:

- **Monthly View**: The calendar displays events for the current month.
- **Navigate Months**: Use the **<** (left) and **>** (right) arrow buttons at the top-left to move between months.
- **Jump to Today**:
  - Click the **"Today"** button to instantly open a popup showing all of today's scheduled events.
  - You can also click the month's name (e.g., "October 2024") to jump the calendar view back to the current month.
- **View a Specific Day's Events**: Click on any date number in the calendar to open a popup listing all events scheduled for that day.

---

## 3. Viewing Event Details

Events on the calendar are shown as small, colored cards.

- **Click on any event card** to open a detailed view. This popup will show you all the important information, such as:
  - Full title and description
  - Date and time
  - Location
  - Faculty or host
  - Associated subject, semester, or section

---

## 4. Mastering the Filters

The real power of PESU Playbook is in its filtering capabilities, located in the sidebar on the left. This allows you to see only the events that matter to you.

You can filter by any combination of the following:

- **Categories & Sub-Types**:
  - Check a main category (e.g., `Academics`, `Placement`) to see all related events.
  - After selecting a category, you can further refine your search by choosing a specific `Sub-Type` (e.g., `ISA`, `Workshop`).

- **Semester/Trimester**:
  - Select your current semester or trimester to narrow down events and subjects relevant to your academic year.

- **Subjects**:
  - Filter the calendar to show events related to specific subjects. This is perfect for tracking exams or assignment deadlines for a particular course.

- **Section**:
  - If your class has multiple sections, you can select yours to see section-specific events.

- **Date Range**:
  - Looking for events in a specific week or timeframe? Use the date range filter to set a custom start and end date.

- **Display Options**:
  - By default, events are colored by their **category**. You can switch the "Color Code by Subject" toggle to color-code them by subject instead, making it easy to spot course-specific events.

- **Clear All Filters**:
  - Click the **"Clear All"** button at the top of the sidebar to reset all filters and return to the full calendar view.

---

We hope this guide helps you stay organized and on top of your academic schedule. Welcome to a more streamlined way of planning your semester!

# Welcome to PESU Playbook!

**Your Ultimate Guide to the Unified Academic Planner**

PESU Playbook is your central hub for all academic events, deadlines, and schedules for your department. This comprehensive guide will walk you through every feature to help you get the most out of the platform.

---

## 1. Getting Started: Finding Your Calendar

When you first visit the PESU Playbook homepage, you are greeted with a welcome screen.

- **Action:** Simply find and click on your department's name from the list.
- **Result:** You will be taken to a dedicated, interactive calendar populated with all the events, exams, and holidays relevant to your department.

This ensures you see a focused view, free from the clutter of other departments' schedules.

---

## 2. Navigating the Interactive Calendar

The main screen is your calendar, designed for easy navigation and quick insights.

#### **Changing Months**
- **Action:** Use the **<** (left arrow) and **>** (right arrow) buttons at the top-left of the calendar.
- **Result:** The calendar will move to the previous or next month, respectively.

#### **Jumping to the Current Month**
- **Action:** Click on the month's name and year (e.g., "October 2024") displayed between the arrow buttons.
- **Result:** The calendar view will instantly jump back to the current month. This is useful if you've navigated far into the past or future.

#### **Viewing Today's Events (Quick View)**
- **Action:** Click the **"Today"** button.
- **Result:** A popup window will appear immediately, listing all events scheduled for the current day. This is the fastest way to see what's on your agenda right now.

#### **Viewing a Specific Day's Events**
- **Action:** Click on any date number within the calendar grid (e.g., click on "15").
- **Result:** A similar popup will appear, showing you all the events scheduled for that specific day.

---

## 3. Understanding Event Details

Events appear as small, colored cards on the calendar. To get the full picture:

- **Action:** Click on any event card.
- **Result:** A detailed dialog box will open, providing comprehensive information, which may include:
  - **Full Title:** The complete name of the event.
  - **Category & Sub-Type:** Helps you understand the event's nature (e.g., Academics - ISA).
  - **Date & Time:** The precise start and end times.
  - **Subject:** If applicable, the course it relates to (e.g., "Corporate Finance").
  - **Semester & Section:** Specific academic groups the event is for.
  - **Location:** The room, hall, or building where the event will take place.
  - **Faculty/Host:** The professor or person in charge.
  - **Description:** Any additional notes, instructions, or context.

---

## 4. Mastering the Filters: Your Personalized View

The sidebar on the left is the most powerful feature of PESU Playbook. It allows you to create a personalized calendar view by showing only the events that are important to you. You can combine any number of filters.

#### **Filter by Categories & Sub-Types**
- **How it works:** This is a two-level filter. First, select a broad category, then narrow it down with a sub-type.
- **Action:**
  1. Check a main category box, like `Academics` or `Placement`. The calendar will update to show all events in that category.
  2. The "Sub-Types" section below it will then populate with options related to your choice (e.g., `ISA`, `Workshop`).
  3. You can then check one or more sub-types to further refine your view.

#### **Filter by Semester/Trimester**
- **How it works:** Shows only events and subjects relevant to a specific academic term.
- **Action:** Check the box for your semester (e.g., `Sem/Trimester 3`). The "Subjects" list below will automatically update to show only courses taught in that semester.

#### **Filter by Subjects**
- **How it works:** Perfect for tracking deadlines or classes for specific courses.
- **Action:** Check the box next to one or more subject names. The calendar will display only the events associated with those subjects.

#### **Filter by Section**
- **How it works:** If your class is divided into sections, this allows you to see events for your specific group.
- **Action:** Check the box for your section (e.g., `Section C`).

#### **Filter by Date Range**
- **How it works:** Allows you to view events within a specific timeframe, like an exam week or a holiday period.
- **Action:**
  1. Click the "Start Date" field to open a mini-calendar and select a beginning date.
  2. Click the "End Date" field to select an end date. The calendar will automatically update.

#### **Change Display Options**
- **How it works:** You can change how events are color-coded on the calendar.
- **Action:** Toggle the **"Color Code by Subject"** switch.
  - When **OFF** (default), events are colored by their main **category** (e.g., all "Academics" events are yellow).
  - When **ON**, events are colored by their associated **subject**, making it easy to spot course-specific deadlines at a glance.

#### **Clearing All Filters**
- **Action:** Click the **"Clear All"** button at the top of the sidebar.
- **Result:** All your filter selections will be removed, and the calendar will return to showing all events for the department.
